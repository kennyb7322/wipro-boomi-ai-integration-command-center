Overview

Role, candidate and why this fits Wipro

A working view of the Wipro role Boomi Developer – AI & Intelligent Integration against the candidate, Kenneth P. Barnes. Every job-description line is mapped to evidence, and each area of the role — design, test, deploy, AI integration, knowledge management — has a pattern you can open and inspect.

What the role asks

  • Translate service designs into Boomi integrations
  • Test, deploy and document them through to production
  • Connect enterprise applications to AI services over REST
  • Apply GenAI to six named integration use cases
  • Keep the service catalog and team knowledge current

What the candidate brings

  • 8 years hands-on Boomi, MuleSoft and Apigee, certified
  • 30+ years of integration and application development
  • 5+ years of production GenAI, RAG and agentic systems
  • Security depth: CISSP, CISM, CCSP; NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001
  • Current Wipro affiliation, and an earlier Wipro engagement in 2017 – 2019

Why it matters to Wipro

  • Wipro is a Boomi Elite+ partner with a joint Digital Integration as-a-Service (DIaaS) offering
  • Boomi, MuleSoft and Apigee are all moving to agent and MCP governance in 2026
  • A developer who can build the integration and the AI controls shortens client pilots

Evidence statements come from the candidate resume. Simulator output and payloads are illustrative; no client data is used.
